NeuroNova – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

NeuroNova – Brain Games respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use our game.

1. Information We Collect

The game may store scores, levels, settings, and progress on your device. If third-party services are integrated, they may collect information according to their own policies.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate the game, save progress, improve gameplay, and fix technical issues.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not intentionally sell personal information. Third-party services used by the app may process information as described in their policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Parents and guardians should review the app's data practices and contact the developer if they have privacy concerns.

5. Data Security

Reasonable steps may be taken to protect information, but no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

If advertising, analytics, or other external services are added, they may collect information under their own privacy policies.

7. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. Updates will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the NeuroNova developer through the app store listing.

This policy must be checked against the app's actual data collection, permissions, advertising, and analytics before publication.
